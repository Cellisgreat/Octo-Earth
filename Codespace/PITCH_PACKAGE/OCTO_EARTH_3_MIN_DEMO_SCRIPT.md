## Recording guardrails

- Do not start training during the take and then present a live reward trace as successful performance.
- If showing any archived comparison, title it **historical pre-hold** and state that it is not evidence of the current active hold.
- Do not use “5% rotation-axis tilt.” Say “5 degrees of orbital-plane inclination, an internal tolerance.”
- Keep the current viewer’s physics-backed motion visually distinct from schematic compressed distances and illustrative Earth weather layers.
- Include readable captions for all caveats; do not rely on voice-over alone.
