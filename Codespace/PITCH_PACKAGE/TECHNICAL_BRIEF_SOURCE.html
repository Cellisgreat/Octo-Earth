OCTO-EARTH · technical briefPitch reference · 27 Oct 2026
A physics-led planetary-mobility prototype

Move the orbit.
Keep the accounting.

OCTO-EARTH studies a hypothetical move of the Earth–Moon system from 1 astronomical unit toward a 2.7 AU circular orbit, with a simplified climate-control objective. Earth moves only through reaction-mass ejections; momentum and energy bookkeeping are explicit.

What this is—and is not

A runnable orbital/RL simulation and visual explanation. It is not a real propulsion design, a certified structure, a climate forecast, a current PPO success report, or evidence that planetary relocation is feasible.

Separate support for E1

  • Earth-adjacent launch ring and eight 2-Earth-radius mass-driver arms; one packet per joint throw.
  • Arm power conditioning and finite reserve.
  • Directional arm radiators and assumed non-damaging operation—neither physically designed.

Separate support for E2

  • Microwave phased arrays and arm-tip rectennas.
  • Collector sail-tilt station keeping and clear sunlight lanes.
  • Factories, Mercury feedstock and construction/repair logistics.

Separate support for E3

Terminator-orbit placement, formation flight, attitude control and pointing. These are not part of the selected mirror-ring enabler label and remain unverified.

What is not hidden: energy originates in modeled sunlight; momentum originates in ejected mass; mirror light is reflected solar energy. No reactionless thrust and no direct RL velocity edit.

Where the numbers come from

The ledger keeps energy sources and sinks explicit. The PV field powers the separate microwave chain; the mirror ring reflects sunlight and is not a power generator.

Energy path

Sunlight → photovoltaic conversion → microwave transmitters → diffraction/capture → arm-tip rectennas → launcher electricity → packet kinetic energy. The books also show collector heat, manufacturing, transmitter heat, beam spill, rectenna heat, launcher heat and any reserve used.

Accounting checks

  • Energy generated by panels is distinct from Earth–Moon orbital energy and from launcher draw; do not conflate these boundaries.
  • Each ejection has a finite packet, launch-energy budget and equal-and-opposite recoil. Fragments are tracked in the high-fidelity model.
  • The mirror ledger separates sunlight incident, light delivered to Earth, light directed past Earth and mirror heat.
  • Reward is not a physical metric. The report presents orbit, energy, mass and climate outputs separately.
Scale warning: 1.114×10²⁵ W is an estimated electrical delivery at the target, while 6.338×10²⁴ W is peak launcher demand. Neither is a measured source nor proof that power electronics or beams can operate safely.

OCTO-EARTH · model and MLSimulation architecture · 5 / 8

Control is downstream of physics

Fast model · training

Hierarchical Kepler propagation advances the Earth–Moon barycentre and Moon orbit; ejections exchange momentum with a finite reservoir. Moon gravity is included, while the Sun’s tidal effect on the Moon is approximated/omitted in this model. Planetary perturbations use documented approximations.

Used for PPO experience collection; not a replacement for high-fidelity checks.

High-fidelity model · physical check

N-body Sun/Earth/Moon with explicit fragments, event handling and energy, momentum, angular-momentum and mass ledgers. Mercury, Venus, Mars and Jupiter are included; planets beyond Jupiter are omitted.

“High-fidelity” is relative to the fast model, not a claim of real-world mission fidelity.

Policy loop

31 observations → PPO policy → 5 actions (throttle, direction angles, ejection speed, mirror-light set point) → physical transition → new orbit, mass, energy, safety and climate metrics. Reward is logged separately from those physical metrics. The agent cannot directly edit Earth velocity or create free energy.

Arrival is not the finish

1 · ACTIVE HOLD

Agent continues making decisions. Orbit and enabled climate must stay in tolerance for the configured consecutive simulated-year hold; a breach resets progress.

2 · PASSIVE COAST

Separate high-fidelity zero-ejection coast after evaluation. The agent does not control this phase; it does not feed back into PPO training.

RL evidence status: current environment is `controlled-target-hold-v1`. No PPO retraining or current-version target-hold evaluation has been run. Legacy checkpoint and first-capture metrics are historical only; no current RL success is claimed.

Core code responsibilities

`octoearth_rl.py` contains configuration, feasibility checks, fast/high-fidelity engines, Gymnasium environment, observation/action/reward mapping, baselines, training/evaluation hooks, conservation checks, guide benchmarks, reports and the API serving the viewer. The PPO trainer is not the physics engine.

Guide benchmarks cover: Lorentz force; closed-loop force in uniform field; Biot–Savart field; eccentric Kepler orbit; five-year energy baseline; Earth–Moon barycentre period; Hohmann transfer; tangential orbital push; cantilever formula screen; blackbody equilibrium.

Structural floor: the guide requires safety factor ≥1.5 for every structural element. Formula-level screens pass their test cases, but the main eight-arm launcher has no material, cross-section, hinge, buckling, dynamic or fatigue analysis and no structural pass/certification.
Planetary safety: Mars gravity and orbit are represented; the configurable shield uses internal tide, Moon-orbit-perturbation and minimum-distance screens (default safe distance ≈0.0246 AU). These are adjustable project settings, not official safety standards; other rulebook thresholds were absent from the supplied PDFs.

Open gates before any feasibility claim

Unresolved engineering

  • Asteroid capture, docking, placement and planetary-scale mass handling.
  • Launcher structure, segment/hinge loads, packet acceleration, shock, atmosphere and Earth surface effects.
  • Directional radiators, extreme waste heat, rectenna intensity and beam spill.
  • Photovoltaic deployment, industrial construction, Mercury mining, transmission and maintenance.
  • Mirror formation/orbits, pointing errors, illumination pattern and life-cycle material supply.

Unresolved model validation

  • Current PPO retraining, multi-seed target-hold evaluation and passive-coast evaluation.
  • Long-term orbit stability near Jupiter resonances and omitted outer-planet effects.
  • Regional climate, atmosphere, oceans, water cycle, biology, spin and seasons.
  • Full structural SF ≥1.5 check and additional rulebook safety limits absent from the supplied PDFs.
  • Instrumentation, faults, communications, control delay and operational resilience.

Next evidence gate

Retrain only after confirming the current environment; evaluate held-out seeds through continuous active hold and the separate passive high-fidelity coast; publish orbital, safety, mass, energy and climate metrics separately from reward; then connect real structural, thermal and launch-shock analyses.
