Goal for each attempt: Earth from 1 AU to 2.7 AU within 10,000 years. That needs Δv = m/s, i.e. an average push of N along the orbit for 10,000 years.

None changes or uses the main asteroid launcher. The 3-D hardware is schematic; the panels identify assumptions and idealizations.
