# OCTO-EARTH environment inventory

This inventory covers the visual-realism copy of the project. The environmental artwork does **not** add bodies, forces, reward terms, or success criteria to the Python simulation. Physical state still comes from the recorded model; the browser renders it. Wall-clock animation effects remain distinct from simulated time.

## Complete object roster

- **Physical model bodies / state:** Sun (gravity and irradiance source); Earth; the physical Earth Moon; the docked project asteroid's remaining mass (part of Earth's existing aggregate model); Mercury, Venus, Mars and Jupiter; recorded ejection packets and fragments. Only their existing Python state and forces drive physical results.
- **Display-only worlds and satellites:** Saturn, Uranus and Neptune; the selected moon markers Phobos, Deimos, Io, Europa, Ganymede, Callisto, Mimas, Enceladus, Tethys, Dione, Rhea, Titan, Iapetus, Miranda, Ariel, Umbriel, Titania, Oberon and Triton. The non-Earth moons and outer planets have schematic paths and no simulated masses/forces; the list is curated, not an exhaustive satellite catalogue.
- **Visible hardware/support objects:** eight-arm launcher ring and grippers, eight arm-tip rectennas, microwave beams and pulses; the five-million solar-collector design (sample graphics plus 12 tracked slot/orbit records); Earth-orbiting climate mirrors; and the 2,400 synthetic main-belt rocks. Rendered fleets/rings are samples or visualizations, not millions of independent bodies.
- **Environment and explanatory graphics:** 14,000 overview star points and denser scene-local starfields; Earth day/night/cloud layers; the selectable global climate map, clouds, winds and ocean-current tracers; magnetic field, solar wind, magnetopause/bow shock, radiation belts, Hill/gravity-well layers; orbit paths, safety rings, labels and HUD markers. These do not feed back into orbital physics.

## Scale and interpretation reminders

- The solar-system overview is not a physical scale model: Sun and planet marker diameters have been enlarged about 1.5× for visibility; Earth, docked asteroid, Moon distance, mirrors, collectors, and small bodies also use stated enlargement/compression. None of this changes any position or orbit. The close-up Earth POV retains its true-scale planet treatment.
- The Earth close-up is true-scale for the Earth, Moon, and the stated physical layers, but its launcher hardware is illustrative. The main asteroid belt appears in the solar-system overview; its graphics are enlarged and are not part of the trajectory solver.
- Decorative star twinkle, Sun-surface animation, cloud drift, asteroid-belt display motion, and arm movement use visual/wall-clock timing. They are not orbital integration, a new reward, or evidence of agent success.
- Existing approximations, unresolved engineering gaps, and claims/caveats in the project Guide and pitch technical brief remain in force. No unsupported RL-success claim is introduced by this visual update.
